📜 HISTORICAL ARCHIVES • FEDERAL CAPITAL TERRITORY

The Genesis of Abuja: Nigeria's Center of Unity

Abuja was conceived not merely as an administrative outpost, but as an enduring symbol of national unity, neutrality, and post-colonial progress. Here is the chronicle of how a virgin savannah beneath the shadow of Aso Rock became Africa's premier planned capital.

⚖️ 1975–1976: Aguda Panel

Following massive congestion in Lagos, General Murtala Mohammed established the Justice Akinola Aguda panel. The committee searched for a neutral location belonging to no dominant ethnic group, with abundant water, fertile soil, and equal accessibility from all 36 states. On February 3, 1976, Decree No. 6 created the Federal Capital Territory.

📐 1979: Kenzo Tange Master Plan

Renowned Pritzker Prize-winning Japanese architect Kenzo Tange and International Planning Associates (IPA) crafted the Abuja Master Plan. The city is designed in concentric crescent crescents, where the Central Business District, National Assembly, Supreme Court, and Presidential Villa sit on a dramatic grand axis directly terminating at the majestic 400-meter monolith of Aso Rock.

🏛️ Dec 12, 1991: The Historic Move

Under the Federal Military Government led by General Ibrahim Badamasi Babangida, the seat of government officially transferred from Dodan Barracks in Lagos to Aso Rock Presidential Villa in Abuja. The city became the operational heartbeat of the nation.

🏙️ The Modern 5 Districts

Maitama (diplomatic embassies & billionaires), Asokoro (high-security seat of power), Wuse 2 (nightlife, fashion & commerce), Garki (markets, banks & civil headquarters), and the Central Business District (National Mosque & Christian Centre).
